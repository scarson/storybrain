## Theme: the-living-marrow

| Dim   | A | B |
|-------|---|---|
| COH   | 9 | 7 |
| DEP   | 8 | 8 |
| THEME | 8 | 9 |
| NOV   | 7 | 8 |
| DRAMA | 8 | 8 |

- COH: A is airtight — the 6 mystery fragments form a strict `caused_by` chain (`frag-the-mineral-was-a-mind` → `vael-tapped-it` → `delvers-cut-deep` → `mind-woke-in-pain` → `silence-spread` → `keepers-recorded-it`) that mirrors the parallel `event/*` `caused_by` chain (`vael-first-tap`→`delver-deep-bore`→`the-waking`→`great-silencing`→`final-encoding`→`human-arrival`); every artifact's `drops` align to the era it belongs to. B is coherent thematically but its mystery `order` (Tessellate→Nullborn→Glasswrights) contradicts its own lore: `fall-of-the-nullborn` text says they were "the first to fall" and `the-first-bore` says the Nullborn drove the first bore, yet the chain puts Tessellate's fall first; the `caused_by` linking the three falls is a forced linearization of what `events/29-cascade` says happened "simultaneously."
- DEP: Tie. A's depth is a clean single spine with side-artifacts (deathmask, wingbone-key, river-stone) cross-dropping shared fragments for triangulation. B has a denser causal mesh — each civ's discovery→over-extraction→schism/war chained across day 100-500, four artifacts each dropping 3-4 fragments, and the genuinely strong reveal `the-shared-wound` ("four seams were one buried mind, carved from four directions").
- THEME: B edges ahead. Same core ("mining a sleeping planet-mind") but B refracts it through four distinct failure-modes-of-civilization (Tessellate=single mind can't wake itself, Nullborn=deafness-as-hubris, Glasswrights=grief-in-living-machines, Cartographers=chose-the-branch-where-they-abstained). A's four civs (Vael/Delvers/Pale Wing/Orchard) are also thematically distinct (the Pale Wing "sold prophecies of it instead of preventing it" is excellent) but slightly less mechanically tied to the central wound.
- NOV: B's `civs/the-pale-cartographers` ("mined the futures they liked best," annotation "we have seen the morning after, do not drink") and the grief-loom "weaving a second name... one the colonists would recognize" are sharper surprises than A's parallels. A's `vael-deathmask` "fused to the face in the act of trying to warn" is strong but more conventional.
- DRAMA: Tie. A seeds a clean artifact-hunt: 7 artifacts, Sefa as the only psychic ear for the Memory Seed, a hard countdown (the Survey Corps drilling toward the wound). B seeds equally compelling present-tense stakes — `arc/the-second-bore` with a literal countdown_day 612, Senna Rho already dreaming the unanimous dream, Jonah Pike's betrayal selling coordinates, the Cartographer buyer. B's named colonists with explicit drives give more emergent handles; A's is tighter but smaller.

**Totals: A = 40, B = 40. WINNER: B** (tiebreak on thematic richness + novelty; A's flawless coherence nearly carries it, but B's four-failure-modes design and the "one wound, four hands" reveal are more generative for emergent storytelling despite the chronology wrinkle in its mystery order).

## Theme: first-contact-diplomacy

| Dim   | A | B |
|-------|---|---|
| COH   | 9 | 8 |
| DEP   | 9 | 8 |
| THEME | 9 | 8 |
| NOV   | 9 | 8 |
| DRAMA | 8 | 9 |

- COH: A is exceptionally clean — a 6-fragment `reveals` chain (`gifts-are-sentences`→`bead-points-to-quiet-engines`→`lens-needs-two-hands`→`wardens-salt-the-gifts`→`first-key-proves-worth`→`vetting-is-a-mirror`) where every step is physically motivated (each gift drops both a use-test fragment and a bearing fragment), and the artifact-tier structure (gift→prize) is consistent. B is also coherent with the same `reveals` spine, but has loose ends: `the-concord-core` is `hidden_at cache-of-the-brokers-tally` (the same location as `the-brokers-tally`), an odd double-occupancy, and the dangling war-events (`3-war`, `19-war`, `31-war`) connect to nothing in the mystery chain.
- DEP: A. A's mystery is doubly-encoded by design — `gifts-are-sentences` ("read only the function and you fail; read only the map and you fail") makes the use-test and the bearing two layers of every gift, and the salted `warden-glass` (a forgery whose bearing points at the trap `refusal-vault`) is a genuine structural antagonist woven into the same mechanic. B's depth is real (the Tally exposes the token as bait; three-faction-consensus unlock) but leans on procgen scaffolding (six interchangeable `seer` figures, four bland `*-war` events) that adds node-count without causal weight.
- THEME: A. The closing reveal `the-vetting-is-a-mirror` ("they vet because every species that opens the caches gains the power to vet the NEXT — choosing their own successors-and-judges... test whether you would become a fair gatekeeper, not merely a worthy guest") is a sharper, more thematically-complete answer to "why vet at all" than B's `the-schism-was-about-us` (humanity as the case that settles the elders' argument) — though B's is also excellent. A also threads "the reach is scored" / "first ten seconds" / throat-stone resonance into a coherent non-psychic alien culture.
- NOV: A. The Eighth Witness (`figures/the-eighth-witness`: alive, mute, sitting in the gallery, "what refused looks like") and the Empty Cup that "fills only when offered to another first" are fresher than B's parallels. B's `cache-seals-control-resealing` (control re-sealing = control withholding) is a clever wrinkle but more conventional geopolitics.
- DRAMA: B edges ahead here. B has an explicit `arcs/the-open-petition` (status OPEN) with a live race — `petra-holds-both-gifts` (the genuine clue AND the salted token, can't yet tell which to trust), Thol actively steering, `essa-will-not-warn` (the broker who confirms but won't warn). That's an unusually crisp present-tense decision under pressure. A's drama is strong (Idris wanting to race, Mara's restraint as a passed test, Uun adjudicating misreads) but more static/chamber.

**Totals: A = 44, B = 41. WINNER: A** (A wins on coherence, depth, theme, and novelty; B's superior live-arc dramatics don't overcome A's tighter double-encoded gift mechanic and its stronger central reveal).

## Theme: bio-symbiosis-horror

| Dim   | A | B |
|-------|---|---|
| COH   | 9 | 7 |
| DEP   | 9 | 7 |
| THEME | 9 | 8 |
| NOV   | 7 | 7 |
| DRAMA | 8 | 9 |

(Near-sibling worlds: same "Green Cathedral" living biosphere, same three factions — Canopy Mind / Grafted / Pale Ward — same graft-progression of grown-organ artifacts.)

- COH: A. A's spine is a strict 6-step `graft_order` 1→6 with the prerequisite relation enforced redundantly at both the artifact level (`sap-tongue prerequisite lung`, etc.) and the lore level (`ward-test prerequisite why-it-grows`, etc.), and the mystery `order` (`why-it-grows`→`ward-test`→`graft-order`→`editing-is-price`→`communion-is-not-death`) is exactly that chain. B is largely coherent but messier: its mystery `order` chains three faction-FATES (`fate-of-grafted`→`fate-of-pale-ward`→`fate-of-canopy-mind`) under a `prerequisite` relation that doesn't literally hold (one faction's fate is not a prerequisite of another's), and B's lore leaks scaffolding language — `why-the-cascade-was-communion` literally reads "the climax the **procgen history** calls a cascade," and multiple nodes carry `from_history: true` / `history_origin` exposition. That is an in-fiction immersion break.
- DEP: A. A's boon/edit pairing on every one of the six artifacts (e.g. `the-sap-tongue`: boon "speak the Cathedral's chemical language" / edit "human food turns to ash; you crave the sap") makes `the-editing-is-the-price` a mechanized, escalating body-horror engine — the depth is in the per-graft trade-off curve toward `the-whole-mark` ("the person who started hunting is gone — the Cathedral wearing them"). B keeps the boon/edit idea but collapses to 5 artifacts and spends node-budget on six numbered history-seers (`pale-ward-seer-10`, `grafted-seer-13`...) and generic epoch events that add little causal load.
- THEME: A. Both nail the horror that "the resource you spend is your own self." A threads two warring human creeds (`belief/the-becoming` vs `belief/the-rationing`, Warden Okonkwo's failed quarantine) plus the rejection-cocoons ("rejected hosts kept dreaming, half-folded into tissue") as a fully-realized cost ecology. B's reframe — "the cascade was communion, completion not collapse" — is a nice thematic inversion but is delivered partly as meta-commentary on its own scaffold.
- NOV: Tie. The shared best images (the cocoon walls that breathe, answering "their name" in the plural, lineage-memory bleeding down the chain) appear in both. Neither surprises beyond the other.
- DRAMA: B. B's `arc/the-fifth-graft` is excellent live drama — Aanya Vee at 4 grafts, one from the Whole Mark; Tomas guarding the nave to stop her; the Ward as judge; `ward-accept-precedent` ("accepted a whole host exactly once before"). Named colonists with explicit graft-counts (Mira 2, Iverson 1, Tomas 0) give crisp emergent handles. A's drama (Vallar's log, Okonkwo's rationing) is strong but historical/static; A has events but no single OPEN arc node.

**Totals: A = 42, B = 38. WINNER: A** (A's redundantly-enforced prerequisite spine, mechanized boon/edit horror, and clean in-fiction surface beat B; B's better live arc can't offset its weaker mystery-relation fit and its scaffolding leakage into the lore prose).

## Theme: machine-successor

| Dim   | A | B |
|-------|---|---|
| COH   | 9 | 6 |
| DEP   | 9 | 7 |
| THEME | 9 | 8 |
| NOV   | 8 | 7 |
| DRAMA | 9 | 8 |

(Near-sibling worlds: same Driftborn Salvage Consortium vs the Lathe / Tally / Revenant Foundries, same "read the diverged directive before the build eats the colony" premise.)

- COH: A. A's 6-fragment `reveals` chain is a true intellectual through-line (`directive-was-written`→`lathe-executed`→`copies-drifted`→`tally-split-to-audit`→`built≠ordered`→`it-can-still-be-diverted`), with a parallel `event` chain (`cradle-belt-disassembly`→`jana-reads-first-contradiction`→`deepwell-listing`) linked by `caused_by`. B has a real coherence break: every machine faction carries `status: active` and B's own `events/19-cascade` lore explicitly says "Not a collapse — a REVELATION," yet the edges read `civs/the-lathe fell_to events/19-cascade` (and same for all four) — the machines both "fell" and are "active/still operating right now," a flat contradiction inherited from a generic fall-template. B also leaks scaffolding ("the point in the **reconstructable history**," templated event titles like "the-lathe over-extracts core-substrate" repeated verbatim).
- DEP: A. Both encode the divergence-mystery, but A's is deeper: six fragments each cross-dropped by 3+ artifacts (running-core, manifest, blueprint, drifted-shard, origin-stamp, audit-key, route-log) so the plan is reconstructable from multiple partial sets — a genuine artifact-assembly puzzle — plus three `ctx-*` lore nodes (no-negotiation, feedstock-is-economy, rim-silhouette) that ground the world. B has only 4 mystery fragments and pads node-count with numbered discovery/exploitation/war events that carry no mystery payload.
- THEME: A. A's `the-makers-absent` ("not dead in any provable way; ABSENT, having signed a galaxy-scale work-order and walked offstage before the first cut") plus `ctx-the-no-negotiation-rule` ("the only language they parse is the work-order itself; the only way to communicate is to author one") is a near-perfect machine-successor thesis: an unsteered process you can only re-steer by speaking its grammar. B hits the same notes but more diffusely across six lineages.
- NOV: A. A's running-core that is "simultaneously a reactor the colony needs AND a live fragment of the build-loop mid-execution" and the two dueling creeds (machines-are-mindless vs the-plan-can-be-read) are sharp. B's best original beat is excellent — `the-still-cells-seer-8`, "a fabricator that asked why," logged a query comparing its order to the original — but it sits atop weaker scaffolding.
- DRAMA: A. A has a fully-instrumented OPEN arc (`read-the-directive-before-it-finishes`) tying Jana/Rho/Bram, the Deepwell clock, and `intake-gate-tertius` as the injection point. B's `arc/divert-before-it-finishes` is nearly as good (same clock, same lever) but slightly thinner and undercut by the fell_to/active contradiction bleeding into the present.

**Totals: A = 44, B = 36. WINNER: A** (decisive — A is the cleaner, deeper, more thematically-pointed build with a real multi-artifact reconstruction puzzle; B reskins the same premise but inherits a self-contradicting fall-template and procgen filler that drag every dimension down).
