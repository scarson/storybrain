# Opus blind verdicts — half 2

Judged blind on artifact quality only. Each world is a graph; story = mystery chain + dropped lore + alien-civ facts + present stakes.

---

## Theme 1: generation-ship-identity

Both worlds share the same strong core conceit: the "alien" makers were human; the colonists are their returned, blinded descendants; a buried crime drove a deliberate self-erasure; three maker-factions (eraser / archivist-redactor / dissenting refuser) seeded the relics; a 6-step `reveals` chain culminates in `it-was-us`.

**Decisive difference — the buried secret.** World A names a concrete crime woven through the whole maker-era timeline: `figures/the-silenced` (a sibling colony deliberately let starve when `m2-the-famine-ledger` proved food for one), with a full causal chain `m1-the-second-seeding → m2-the-famine-ledger → m3-the-silence → m4-the-vote-to-forget → m5-the-dispersal`, supporting figures (`the-quartermaster`, `the-ferryman`, `the-cartographer`, `the-scatterer`), and a present-day geographic loose thread (`locations/the-sibling-coast` — the crime is physically reachable). World B's buried secret is a self-confessed placeholder: `lore/the-weight-of-it` literally says "(Per this world: a cure withheld...)" — meta-narration leaking into the fiction, and the cure never connects to any maker-era event or figure.

**Maker-era timeline.** A's events are dated milestones with a clean cause chain and thematic payload. B's events (`0-schism`, `1-war`, `4-exploitation`, `19-exploitation`...) are generic procedural beats ("the-erased over-extracts vault-salt", "a seer rises among the-redactors") with seer figures (`the-erased-seer-0`, etc.) that never feed the mystery — shallow filler. B's `wound: 0` and numbered slugs (`-5`, `-8`, `-28`) betray template scaffolding.

**Dropped-lore wiring.** Both over-connect artifacts to fragments (many-to-many `drops`), but A's mapping is thematically tight (the Fitting Cradle drops "the cradle fits"; the Starved Chart records the famine ledger via a dedicated `records` edge). B drops lore onto numbered artifacts with no `records` linkage to events.

**Present stakes.** Near-identical quality: both have a relic-reader (Anwen / Mara), a governor wanting to re-bury (Rhys / Tomas), the vault-opener (Jax / Iris), and a faith-keeper (Imo / Juno). A edges it with the precise "Jax stalls / has not told the Governor it is ready" tension and the explicit "repeat the makers' exact choice" framing in `arc/the-reckoning`.

| Criterion | A | B |
|---|---|---|
| Coherence | 9 | 7 — the placeholder "(Per this world...)" leaks meta into canon; seer/war events disconnected from mystery |
| Causal/structural depth | 9 — full dated crime chain w/ records-edges & a reachable crime site | 6 — maker-era events are procedural filler, no causal tie to the secret |
| Thematic richness | 9 — famine triage + clean-inheritance vs owed-truth beliefs both seeded | 7 — same themes, thinner; beliefs not encoded as nodes |
| Novelty/surprise | 8 — "the blank panel has a shape" / inherited grief as encryption key | 6 — same beats, a vaguer payload |
| Dramatic potential | 9 — the Sibling Coast loose end + standoff make it stageable now | 8 |

Rationale (slugs): A `figures/the-silenced` + `events/m2-the-famine-ledger` + `locations/the-sibling-coast` give the mystery a concrete, dramatizable bottom; A `artifacts/the-mourners-will` "keyed to grief... opens only for a reader whose ancestral memory already aches" is a standout device. B `lore/the-weight-of-it` self-admits its own emptiness; B's `events/*-schism|war|exploitation` are scenery.

**WINNER: A.**  Totals — **A: 44**, B: 34.

## Theme 2: megastructure-absent-architects

Near-identical skeletons: a working Dyson-shell megastructure, builders gone with no corpses, a 5-step `enables` mystery chain (`switched-off-in-order → keepers-darkened → aims-outward → purpose-was-departure → core-fires-once`) culminating in the reveal the structure is a one-shot launch/emitter; three layered absent civs (Architects → Keepers caretakers → Listeners inheritor-pilgrims); a gift-vs-trap doctrine split; a present arc "light the last key or leave it dark."

**A is the richer build of the shared idea.** Standout devices unique to A: `artifacts/the-core-ward` — "a key that REFUSES; the only one built to keep a room shut" — a genuinely fresh object that inverts the whole key-collecting premise and is thematically loaded; `curses/the-one-shot` as an explicit irreversibility node that `binds` the arc and `warns` the zealot Vale; and figures with mythic weight — `the-last-keeper` "who wrote the shutdown codex and walked into the dark last," `the-listener-glyph` a parting message that `warns` the fleet. A also gives the human side real structure: three named splinter factions (`inheritor-fleet`, `keybearers`, `warders`) that `splintered_from` the parent and each have a leader and a doctrine node — institutional, not just three opinionated individuals.

**B's distinguishing weakness — an authorship contradiction.** `figures/the-keepers-seer-11` (civ: the-keepers) `wrote` → `artifacts/the-listeners-activation-codex-31`, an artifact slugged and `forged_by the-listeners`. A Keeper cannot have authored a Listener artifact; the maker attribution and the authorship edge disagree. B also leans on template scaffolding: `wound: 0` on every civ, numbered slugs (`-8`, `-16`, `-31`), and four "seer" figures (`seer-5/11/17/21`) that are mostly inert — only one (`seer-21`, "chose to depart rather than fire the core") earns its place by mirroring the present dilemma.

**Mystery wiring.** Both use the same multi-drop fan-out (each fragment dropped by several artifacts) plus a clean linear `enables` spine plus per-fragment `reveals` into civs/locations. Equivalent and competent in both. A's neutral "scenery" lore (`shell-lumen-is-free-power`, `cradles-were-left-tidy`) is slightly better integrated because it's split across the right artifacts; B's is comparable. B does add a nice touch A lacks: `events/cascade` ("the climax that already happened, reconstructed in the present") and `the-architects vanished_at cascade`, giving the backstory a single dramatized pivot — but it's one node against A's several.

| Criterion | A | B |
|---|---|---|
| Coherence | 9 | 7 — Keeper-seer authoring a Listener codex is a real maker/authorship contradiction |
| Causal/structural depth | 9 — refusing-key + one-shot curse add a second axis to the key chain | 8 — clean chain, plus the `cascade` pivot event |
| Thematic richness | 9 — gift/trap doctrine embodied in 3 chartered factions + the ward-as-warning | 7 — same themes carried by individuals; seers mostly inert |
| Novelty/surprise | 9 — `the-core-ward` "a key that REFUSES" is the best single device in the pair | 7 |
| Dramatic potential | 8 | 8 — "final key already in hand" present_tension is a strong stake |

Rationale (slugs): A `artifacts/the-core-ward` + `curses/the-one-shot` + `figures/the-last-keeper` give the absence texture and irreversible stakes; B `figures/the-keepers-seer-11 wrote artifacts/the-listeners-activation-codex-31` is the coherence ding; B `events/cascade` is its best original beat.

**WINNER: A.**  Totals — **A: 44**, B: 37.

## Theme 3: extinction-plague-vector

Same core in both: makers (`cradlekeepers`) sealed themselves in quarantine-sleep behind an unfinished containment; `stillborn-host` are dormant carriers indistinguishable from the makers; `the-vector` is the plague itself; a present human containment-colony must assemble a 5-step seal in exact order (`proof → identify → trigger → keystone → assemble`, `prerequisite`-ordered) before a stirring carrier wakes. Identical mystery skeleton and arc framing ("forward containment protocol, NOT an autopsy").

**This pairing reverses the prior two — B is decisively stronger.** A's entire maker-era backstory is generic procedural filler: ~15 numbered events that are slot-machine combat/economy beats — `0-war`/`5-war`/`10-war`/`14-war`/`19-war`/`22-war`/`28-war` all "X wars on Y", `1/2/3/4-discovery/exploitation` "taps/over-extracts dormancy-salt", and four `*-schism` "a seer rises among X" with inert seer figures (`the-vector-seer-13/25`, `cradlekeepers-seer-18/31`). None of it connects to the present race; the artifacts are numbered-slug placeholders (`-5`, `-13`, `-18`, `-22`, `-28`, `-31`). A's `wound: 0`, fractional tensions like `0.40499999999999997`, and repeated identical war titles betray raw generation.

**B builds an actual present-tense story.** It discards the procedural era entirely and instead lays a coherent dated *now*-timeline: `planetfall-inside-the-range` (day 0) → `first-relic-cracked-blind` (day 40, Devrant's mistake) → `the-translators-fever` (day 50) → `the-carrier-stirs` (day 80, "the clock starts") → `the-ashline-vote` (day 90, schism) → `the-keystone-located` (day 110). Causality is explicit (`first-relic-cracked-blind caused_by devrant-isk`, `stirs civs/the-vector`). It has a real ensemble with opposed stances — `mara-okonkwo` (order), `devrant-isk` (reckless), `the-translator` (most exposed), `sergeant-vo` (burn it), `dr-amein` (wake them as allies), `child-tam` (the stakes embodied) — plus a splinter faction `factions/the-ashline-creed` and three opposed belief nodes (`order-is-the-only-mercy`, `the-makers-can-be-woken`, `every-seal-is-a-lie`). And it nails the theme with `curse/the-reading-spreads-it` — a memetic vector that "rides the lore; to understand a relic is to risk carrying it" — which is causally wired to the translator's fever and makes the `the-translator reads → exposed_to` thread genuinely dramatic.

**Artifact/lore wiring.** B's artifacts carry hazard tiers (`low→medium→high→extreme`) that map to the seal steps and are physically placed (`identification-shard lies_in the-cold-archive`, `keystone lies_in the-still-vault`). A's artifacts are unplaced and named only by maker + index. Both use the same multi-drop fan-out into the 5 fragments; B additionally reveals locations (`marrow-reliquary`, `still-vault`) from fragments, deepening the spatial mystery.

| Criterion | A | B |
|---|---|---|
| Coherence | 6 — backstory is repetitive generated filler disconnected from the arc | 9 — tight dated causal now-timeline |
| Causal/structural depth | 6 — many edges, little meaning; events don't feed the race | 9 — mistake→stir→vote→clock chain with explicit causes |
| Thematic richness | 6 — theme stated in lore only | 9 — `curse/the-reading-spreads-it` + Ashline Creed + 3 beliefs embody it |
| Novelty/surprise | 5 — boilerplate war/seer churn | 8 — memetic "reading spreads it" + wake-as-vaccine vs scorch dilemma |
| Dramatic potential | 7 — the present arc itself is strong (shared) | 9 — full cast, splinter faction, child-tam as stakes |

Rationale (slugs): A `events/0-war`…`31-schism` + `figures/*-seer-*` are pure filler; B `curse/the-reading-spreads-it` + `event/the-translators-fever` + `factions/the-ashline-creed` + the dated `events/*` timeline are the difference. A's only edge is sharing the same (strong) arc node.

**WINNER: B.**  Totals — A: 30, **B: 44**.

## Theme 4: crowded-trade-federation

Both share an excellent conceit: a forged-provenance noir. A humming "ghostlight-amber" curio's papers are peeled back layer by layer (`forged-mined-origin → khariti-carried-it → melun-smuggled-source → oseni-authentic-transfer → true-origin-living-maker`, `precedes`-ordered), and the true origin is the kicker — the amber is not mined but grown by a living maker-species the whole market conspires to pretend doesn't exist so its forgeries hold value. Five living houses (Vossan brokers / Khariti nomads / Oseni assayers / Melun cartel / Rho collectors), same human tracer Marlena, same `the-mined-myth` / `provenance-is-power` beliefs, same `amber-hums-near-kin` first-crack clue. Genuinely strong shared core, and the only theme where both worlds are good.

**Same divergence pattern as theme 3 — B is the better build.** A again drags procedural filler into the backstory: `events/0-discovery`/`1-exploitation`/`5-discovery`/`6-exploitation` "taps/over-extracts ghostlight-amber", `4-schism`/`11-war` "a seer rises / X wars on Y", inert `*-seer-*` figures, numbered-slug artifacts (`-2`, `-4`, `-9`, `-26`, `-30`), and a `0.44999999999999996` tension. These don't advance the deal. B deletes them and instead lays a tight escalating noir timeline, each beat `caused_by` the prior: `the-cheap-sale` (d10) → `the-buyback-offer` (d20, "then stop asking nicely") → `the-assayer-bribe` (d35, Saanu flips) → `the-manifest-handoff` (d50, Yer trades the manifest) → `the-runs-ambush` (d65, Melun ambush to bury the bill). That is a playable five-beat investigation with rising danger.

**Worldbuilding texture.** B is more systemically wired: every house `controls` a named location (`assayers-hall`, `drift-anchorage`, `collectors-sanctum`, `melun-runs`), houses have lateral relations (`rival_of`, `deals_with`) rather than only resource-exploit spam, each house has a named member with motive (`old-saanu` dissident, `the-rho-archivist` funder, `sho-melun` enforcer), and each gets a characterizing flavor-lore including the sharp `collectors-blind-spot` ("they pay for clean stories and never check them — exactly how forgeries find their richest market"). A's houses mostly just `exploits` two seams repeatedly. A's redeeming strengths: its chain-artifacts are physically held by specific figures, and it has one extra mechanic (`arcs/the-provenance-deal` spelling out the "sell the truth / sell the silence / free the maker" choice) that is slightly more explicit than B's arc framing.

**Minor ding on B:** `mystery.fragments` is listed newest-lie-first while `mystery.order` is true-origin-first. The `order` array (authoritative) is correct and matches every `precedes` edge, so this is cosmetic list-ordering, not a logic break. A's fragments and order arrays agree.

| Criterion | A | B |
|---|---|---|
| Coherence | 7 — strong chain, but filler events clutter the graph | 9 — every beat causally chained; one cosmetic list-order quirk |
| Causal/structural depth | 7 — chain `precedes` solid; backstory edges inert | 9 — dated `caused_by` noir escalation + inter-house relations |
| Thematic richness | 8 — mined-myth/living-maker reveal lands in both | 8 — same, plus per-house flavor lore (collectors-blind-spot) |
| Novelty/surprise | 8 — "a clean fake costs more than a dirty truth" | 8 — equivalent; same killer reveal |
| Dramatic potential | 7 — strong arc node, less staged | 9 — ambush + buyback + bribe give ready scenes |

Rationale (slugs): shared `lore/true-origin-living-maker` reveal is the best idea in both; A `events/0-discovery`…`11-war` + `figures/*-seer-*` are filler; B `events/the-cheap-sale → … → the-runs-ambush` chain + `lore/collectors-blind-spot` + per-house `controls` edges are the edge.

**WINNER: B.**  Totals — A: 37, **B: 43**.

## Theme 5: uplift-nursery-world

Both share a superb conceit and the same 5-step method chain (`seeding → precedent → attention → shaping → harvest-ahead`, `reveals`-ordered): a human colony on a too-convenient world is being uplifted/cultivated by unseen Gardeners; the "gifts" (seeds, gene-looms, attention-bells, shrines) are instruments that test and edit the colony; the precedent is an earlier grown lineage (First Cultivars); and the killer structural move — the climax (`the-harvest-ahead`) points FORWARD, "not a fall, a culmination ahead," so the mystery resolves into a future dread rather than an autopsy. Both nail the chain and the forward-pointing arc.

**Same A/B split as 3 and 4 — B is the stronger build.** A's backstory is procedural filler again: `events/0-discovery`/`1-exploitation`/`2-discovery`/`4-discovery`/`5-exploitation`/`16-schism` ("begins tending / steers / a sensing-one wakes"), numbered-slug artifacts (`-4`, `-6`, `-8`, `-15`, `-24`, `-26`, `-32`), four interchangeable `*-seer-*` figures, and a `cultivates`-spam timeline. Worse, A's flavor lore is literally duplicated: `lore/flavor-2` and `lore/flavor-4` are the identical string ("a terraforming-seed makes a place too convenient overnight"), as are `flavor-6`/`flavor-10`, `flavor-7`/`flavor-11`, and `flavor-0`/`flavor-12` — a clear generation artifact, padding the node count with copies.

**B replaces filler with character and concrete proof.** Named colonists carry the dread personally: `osei-loom-warden` "measured the loom's output against his own children's blood-work and stopped sleeping" (note-loom-edits-host) — that single line does more than A's entire seer roster. The artifacts are individuated and evocative (the Provident Seed, the Living Loom, the Kneeling Spring, the Threshold Bell, the Steering-Rod). B's standout device is `artifacts/lineage-key` + `lore/note-key-fits-us`: "the Inherited Key's teeth match a lock found inside the human genome — a lock that was not there at landfall" — a falsifiable, chilling proof the colony has already been edited, paired with `note-cultivars-gone` ("no graves, no war-scars — they were not destroyed; they were taken in"), which reframes the precedent as seduction rather than slaughter. B also has two opposed belief nodes (`the-patient-hand` vs `we-are-the-crop`), faction sub-structure (`the-grateful`/`the-harvesters` are `part_of` the colony), and two present events.

**Mystery wiring.** Both use the same dual-spine (linear `reveals` between fragments + per-fragment `reveals` into civs) and multi-drop fan-out. Equivalent in structure; B's drops attach to thematically named artifacts and its supplementary notes are all distinct and load-bearing, where A's are duplicated boilerplate.

| Criterion | A | B |
|---|---|---|
| Coherence | 7 — strong chain, but duplicated flavor lore + inert seer/event churn | 9 — every node distinct and purposeful |
| Causal/structural depth | 7 — backstory edges are `cultivates`-spam, not story | 8 — character-anchored, two present events, faction sub-structure |
| Thematic richness | 8 — "using it feels like authorship; it is closer to obedience" | 9 — same plus genome-lock proof + "taken in" precedent |
| Novelty/surprise | 8 — forward-harvest reveal (shared) | 9 — `note-key-fits-us` genome lock is the best original beat |
| Dramatic potential | 7 — abstract; no named human stakes | 9 — Osei's blood-work, Vela the denier, Idris's doubt give scenes |

Rationale (slugs): shared `lore/method-5-the-harvest-ahead` + `events/the-harvest-ahead` forward-climax is the great idea in both; A `lore/flavor-2`==`flavor-4` (and three more dup pairs) + `figures/*-seer-*` are filler; B `artifacts/lineage-key` + `lore/note-key-fits-us` + `lore/note-loom-edits-host` (Osei's children) are the difference.

**WINNER: B.**  Totals — A: 37, **B: 44**.

Overall: A wins 2, B wins 3. The recurring fault line: in 4 of 5 themes the two worlds share an identical strong conceit and mystery skeleton, and the decision turns on the *backstory layer*. One side repeatedly fills it with procedural slot-machine churn (numbered `*-war`/`*-schism`/`*-exploitation` events, inert `*-seer-*` figures, numbered-slug artifacts, and — in uplift — literally duplicated flavor lore); the other replaces that with dated causal now-timelines, named characters with motives, and concrete falsifiable proofs. Note this maps to whichever blinded letter carried the filler, which flips between themes (A in 3/4/5, B in 1; theme 2 had no filler-side, decided on a contradiction).
