# Blind Judge Verdicts — opus, half 3 (themes: machine-successor, time-war)

Judging artifact quality only. Two worlds per theme (A, B). Scores 0–10 on
Coherence, Causal/Structural Depth, Thematic Richness, Novelty/Surprise,
Dramatic Potential.

---

## Theme: machine-successor

### Mystery trace

- **A**: 6-fragment `reveals` spine, frag-one→…→frag-six, a clean intellectual
  arc: *a directive was written → the Lathe executed it faithfully → copies
  drifted → the Tally split off to audit → the built thing is not the ordered
  thing → it can still be diverted*. Each fragment also `reveals` a distinct
  faction/location, and the spine doubles as the detective logic AND the lever
  (`frag-six-it-can-still-be-diverted` makes reading the plan the way to change
  it). Artifacts each drop 3 fragments with sensible attribution (origin-stamp →
  frag-one + frag-five; drifted-directive-shard → frag-three pins the mutation).
- **B**: 4-fragment `reveals` spine (`fate-of-the-lathe`…`fate-of-the-spindle-cohort`),
  read in reverse-reveal order; same conceptual payload (original intent →
  first drift → mechanism → built≠ordered) but compressed and with a structural
  oddity: every fragment ALSO `reveals events/19-cascade` / its own civ, and
  each civ `fell_to events/19-cascade` while simultaneously being `status:
  active` — "the directive diverged" is reframed as a revelation not a fall,
  which is clever, but the `fell_to` edge fights the active-status fiction.

### Findings

- **A coherence is tight.** Named events form a real `caused_by` chain:
  `the-cradle-belt-disassembly` → `jana-reads-the-first-contradiction` →
  `the-deepwell-listing` — trauma → discovery → clock. Two dueling beliefs
  (`the-machines-are-mindless` vs `the-plan-can-be-read`) anchored to Rho and
  Jana give the arc its human axis. Context-lore (`ctx-feedstock-is-the-economy`,
  `ctx-the-no-negotiation-rule`, `ctx-the-rim-silhouette`) each justify a
  worldbuilding pillar. Almost no inert nodes.
- **A flaw (minor):** Old Bram is `located_at` both `revenant-yard-ix` AND
  `the-quiet-foundry`; harmless. The `sought_by` direction (artifact sought_by
  colonist reversed as colonist sought_by artifact) reads oddly but is legible.
- **B is filler-heavy.** ~11 templated event nodes (`events/1-exploitation:
  the-lathe over-extracts core-substrate`, `events/6`, `events/10`, …) all
  stamped `day:100`/`day:200`, generic `discovery/exploitation/war` kinds, many
  inert (only `involves`/`caused_by` to each other, no narrative payoff).
- **B attribution contradictions.** Artifact maker fields fight the fiction:
  `the-spindle-cohort` forges an `audit-key-relic` (a *Tally* reconciliation
  token); `the-lathe` forges both a `founding-work-order` ("says what was
  actually asked for" — that is the absent-makers'/Tally's artifact) AND an
  audit-key. `the-revenant-foundries` (corrupted copies) forge a *founding*
  work-order. The bracketed names (`Audit-Key Relic (the-lathe)`) expose the
  template. A's `the-origin-stamp forged_by the-makers-absent` is the correct
  thematic move B fumbles.
- **B's genuine bright spot:** `figures/the-still-cells-seer-8` — "a fabricator
  that asked why," a halted cell that logged a query comparing its order to the
  original. Real novelty and the single best image in either world. But it is
  nearly inert: it connects only to one schism event and is not wired into the
  mystery spine.
- **B missing pieces vs A:** no opposing-belief structure (only Jana's creed,
  no `machines-are-mindless` node despite Rho's bio asserting it), no named
  trauma event analogous to the Cradle Belt Disassembly, fewer real locations
  (seams are generic resource nodes with uniform `tension`/`wound:0`).

### A vs B

| Axis | A | B | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coherence | 9 | 6 | A: causal event chain + correct artifact attribution. B: maker/archetype contradictions, active-yet-`fell_to` |
| Causal/Structural depth | 9 | 6 | A: `caused_by` trauma→clock spine, dual beliefs. B: templated exploitation events are structural noise |
| Thematic richness | 9 | 7 | A: feedstock-economy + no-negotiation + lever lore each load-bearing. B: same themes, thinner, contradicted by attribution |
| Novelty/surprise | 7 | 7 | A: "author a work-order is the only language" is sharp. B: the seer "that asked why" is the strongest single image, but stranded |
| Dramatic potential | 9 | 6 | A: Rho-vs-Jana-vs-Bram three-way + dated clock. B: arc fine but events inert, no human-belief conflict |

**Totals: A = 43, B = 32. WINNER: A.**

## Theme: time-war

### Mystery trace

- **A**: 5-fragment `precedes` spine. Stored `order` runs
  `the-war-is-still-being-decided` → `…archived-every-ending` →
  `the-tenses-collapsed` → `…revised-the-opening` → `…struck-from-downstream`,
  i.e. effect-first / latest-truth-first, which is the whole conceit ("the relic
  you place last picks the winner"). The `precedes` edges are deliberately
  *reversed* against intuition and the framing makes that a feature: a war read
  back-to-front. Five extra non-spine lore fragments (envoy-bootstrap,
  medal-as-obligation, eraser-rewrites-finds, treaty-proves-deleted-peace,
  aelthar-may-never-have-been) each illuminate one anachronism artifact.
- **B**: 5-fragment `precedes` spine, structurally identical pattern
  (`the-war-has-not-finished-happening` first), same payload: war runs backward
  → undated edited the opening → cause/effect unpinned → oncewill archived
  endings → still resolving. Also carries strong non-spine lore
  (`the-first-find-was-the-ending`, `the-reply-fits-no-question`,
  `anachronism-predates-its-maker`, `the-colony-is-a-move`).

### Findings

- **Both worlds share the same excellent core conceit** (a war fought across
  tense; sequencing the relics IS firing the last shot; the expedition's
  catalogue is itself a battleground). This is high-concept and well-executed in
  both — the strongest theme of the four.
- **A's artifacts are bespoke and iconic.** `the-medal-for-a-battle-not-yet-fought`,
  `the-envoys-older-skull` (dated centuries before its makers), `the-eraser-stone`,
  `the-treaty-signed-by-the-dead`, `the-first-shot-that-came-last`. Each name is
  a self-contained paradox; each drops lore that pays the name off
  (`the-medal-rewards-the-unfought` = "a promise the timeline is obligated to
  keep"). The `figures/the-recurring-envoy` "sent before his senders existed"
  with the skull `forged_by` both the envoy AND the Aelthar is a tight
  bootstrap-paradox loop.
- **A's human layer is concrete.** Okonkwo's wall-chart that re-pins itself,
  Reyes wanting to flee, Dane (`the-touched-surveyor`) remembering the
  expedition's *own* ending and refusing to name it. `events/now-0` (sequence
  inverts when first-shot is dated last) and `now-1` (camp flickers to
  battlefield) are specific dramatic beats. The arc holds "five of six finds;
  the sixth decides the war" — a literal cliffhanger.
- **A flaw:** dense `drops` fan-out means a few lore nodes are reached from
  multiple artifacts (`the-account-has-three-endings` from two), and
  `precognition-is-the-war-pressing-through` is dropped by three artifacts —
  slight redundancy, but thematically defensible (the leak is everywhere).
- **B's artifacts are template-named.** `the-oncewill-relic-that-remembers-a-future-4`,
  `the-undated-anachronism-37`, `the-oncewill-effect-before-cause-20` — the
  archetype + maker + integer ID are exposed in the slug AND the displayed name.
  Two `relic-that-remembers-a-future`, two `effect-before-cause`, two
  `anachronism` — duplicated archetypes with only `note` text to distinguish
  them. The notes are good ("a reply with no message," "the last find… teaches
  there was no 'last' at all") but the objects lack A's proper-noun identity.
- **B's faction naming is stronger in one way:** the-Erevan / the-Undated /
  the-Oncewill are crisp coined names; A uses descriptive handles
  (the-Aelthar is named, but "the-witnesses-who-remember-ahead" is a phrase, not
  a name). Slight edge to B on faction nomenclature.
- **B has more inert/duplicate scaffolding.** Two near-identical
  `figures/the-undated-seer-1` and `…-seer-27` (schismatic seers), only loosely
  wired; `the-oncewill-witness` owns one relic. A's three figures
  (envoy, last-editor, witness-who-stayed) are each distinct roles tied to a
  faction and, for the envoy/witness, to a specific paradox artifact.
- **B coherence nit:** `anachronism-predates-its-maker` is dropped by both
  anachronism-24 and anachronism-37 and `reveals civs/the-erevan`, while the
  same fragment's text says "the Undated may have authored the Erevan" — the
  reveal target is arguable but defensible. Minor.

### A vs B

| Axis | A | B | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coherence | 9 | 8 | Both clean; A's bespoke artifacts map 1:1 to paradox lore, B leans on `note` to disambiguate duplicate archetypes |
| Causal/Structural depth | 9 | 8 | Both use reversed `precedes` as a feature; A adds the envoy bootstrap loop + concrete `now-` beats; B's seers are near-duplicate filler |
| Thematic richness | 9 | 9 | Shared top-tier conceit; A's treaty/medal/skull and B's "the-colony-is-a-move"/"reply-fits-no-question" are both excellent |
| Novelty/surprise | 9 | 8 | Both surprising; A's named paradox-objects land harder than B's templated `-37` relics |
| Dramatic potential | 9 | 8 | A: Dane refusing to name the ending + "five of six finds" cliffhanger is sharper; B's Vash-races-the-edit is good but thinner human texture |

**Totals: A = 45, B = 41. WINNER: A.**
