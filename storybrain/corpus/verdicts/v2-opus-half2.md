# Blind Judging — v2 Opus (half 2)

Five themes, two blinded worlds each (A vs B). Scored 0–10 per axis:
COHERENCE, CAUSAL/STRUCTURAL DEPTH, THEMATIC RICHNESS, NOVELTY/SURPRISE, DRAMATIC POTENTIAL.

---

## Theme 1 — generation-ship-identity

Both worlds share the same core conceit: colonists ("Foundlings"/"Returnees") who
think they are first-arrivals are in fact the scattered descendants of a vanished
human maker-civ that erased itself on purpose; relic-borne ancestral memory
reassembles the buried crime in six ordered fragments ending on `lore/it-was-us`,
and the climax is whether to forget again. The differentiator is what's actually
buried and how densely the prehistory is built out.

| Axis | World A | World B |
|---|---|---|
| Coherence | 8 | 8 |
| Causal/Structural Depth | 9 | 7 |
| Thematic Richness | 9 | 8 |
| Novelty/Surprise | 8 | 7 |
| Dramatic Potential | 9 | 8 |
| **Total** | **43** | **38** |

**Rationale (A):**
- Coherence: clean six-step `reveals` chain on the mystery; present-day fork
  (`colonists/dr-anwen-cole` vs `colonists/governor-rhys-tan`, with
  `colonists/jax-ferro` as the hand on `artifacts/the-warm-vault`) maps exactly
  onto the maker-era schism (`factions/the-mourners` vs `factions/the-cleaners`).
  Minor nit: `lore/the-choice-made` uses bare `about:"the-silenced"` while siblings
  use full `figures/` slugs; harmless inconsistency.
- Depth: the buried crime is concrete and load-bearing — `figures/the-silenced`,
  a sibling colony starved to death so this one could live — and it gets a full
  prehistory event-spine `events/m1-the-second-seeding`→`m5-the-dispersal` with a
  named functionary per beat (`figures/the-quartermaster`, `the-ferryman`,
  `the-cartographer`, `the-scatterer`) and a physical site
  (`locations/the-sibling-coast`) where a relic-blind colonist could trip over the
  crime early. This is real structural scaffolding, not decoration.
- Richness: the two doctrines (`beliefs/the-clean-inheritance` vs
  `beliefs/the-owed-truth`) are genuinely opposed moral philosophies that the
  present-day fight re-litigates; "innocence kept by forgetting" is a sharp theme.
- Novelty: self-erasure-as-penance plus the deliberate flaw in the seal
  (`figures/the-first-cleaner` carving a door "as if some part of him could not
  bear to be obeyed completely") is a strong twist.
- Drama: `arc/the-reckoning` is explicitly UNRESOLVED with the vault openable now;
  the standoff (`events/now-2-order`) is live.
- Weakness: `locations/cistern-stone-cut` self-describes as "scenery, not stakes" —
  an honest inert node; the drops-edges are heavily cross-wired (many artifacts
  drop many fragments), mild redundancy.

**Rationale (B):**
- Coherence: equally clean six-step chain and a tidy three-lineage maker structure
  (`civs/the-erased`/`the-redactors`/`the-refusers`); prose is the strongest of the
  pair (`civs/the-erased` detail). Present-day fork mirrors the past well.
- Depth: solid prehistory spine (`events/the-question-raised`→`the-scattering`) but
  thinner — the named figures are one-per-caste archetypes, no equivalent of A's
  victim-site or functionary cast.
- Richness: good, but the **central secret is the weak link**:
  `lore/the-weight-of-the-secret` literally hedges in-text — "(Per this world: a
  cure withheld because it could never be shared fairly...)". The parenthetical
  self-flag reads as placeholder/uncertain authorship, and a withheld cure is a
  far weaker motive for civilizational self-erasure than A's genocide-by-omission.
- Novelty: the deliberate-flaw beat (`figures/last-editor`) duplicates A's almost
  exactly; less surprising because B got there second on the page-feel and the
  payload is softer.
- Drama: `arc/the-second-choice` is open and well-staged (`events/the-vault-standoff`).
- Inert/filler: `locations/echo-resin-seam` and `locations/vault-salt-flats` both
  self-describe as incidental ("the resource was never the point"); same honest-inert
  pattern as A but two of them.

**WINNER: A** (43 vs 38) — same premise executed with a concrete, motive-strong
buried crime and a denser, victim-anchored prehistory; B is better-written
sentence-for-sentence but its hedged central secret undercuts the whole mystery.

## Theme 2 — megastructure-absent-architects

Paired variants again: humans wake inside a working Dyson shell whose builders
(`civs/the-architects`) vanished without corpses; a successor caretaker caste
(`civs/the-keepers`) powered the rings down in deliberate reverse-order; an
earlier inheritor species (the First Listeners / the Listeners) woke part of it,
learned the aim, and left. A 5-fragment `enables` chain reveals the shell is an
emitter and the core fires a one-time departure. The live fork is finish-it
(gift) vs leave-it-dark (trap). Discriminator: how well the Listeners' precedent
and the irreversibility are wired into the present-day drama.

| Axis | World A | World B |
|---|---|---|
| Coherence | 8 | 9 |
| Causal/Structural Depth | 8 | 9 |
| Thematic Richness | 7 | 8 |
| Novelty/Surprise | 8 | 7 |
| Dramatic Potential | 7 | 9 |
| **Total** | **38** | **42** |

**Rationale (A):**
- Coherence: solid artifact-gating spine (`the-cradle-sigil`→`the-threshold-key`→
  `the-keepers-codex`→`the-listeners-relay`→`the-final-fragment`) and a clean
  5-step `enables` mystery. Structural oddity: `artifacts/the-core-ward` sits
  OUTSIDE the enables-chain yet drops the final fragment
  (`lore/the-core-is-the-engine-of-leaving`) — a side-branch that slightly muddies
  the linear gate.
- Depth: good three-civ stack (`civs/the-architects`/`the-keepers`/
  `the-first-listeners`), but `the-first-listeners` are underbuilt ("woke pieces…
  left without explanation") — the refusal precedent that should power the Warder
  case is asserted, not dramatized.
- Richness: the gift-vs-trap doctrines (`beliefs/the-gift-doctrine` vs
  `beliefs/the-trap-doctrine`) are clear; three human factions
  (`the-keybearers`/`the-warders`) give it political texture.
- Novelty: best-in-pair touches — `artifacts/the-core-ward` is "a key that
  REFUSES; the only one built to keep a room shut," and `curses/the-one-shot` is a
  distinct irreversibility node. The "shutdown read backwards = build sequence"
  (`artifacts/the-keepers-codex`) is elegant.
- Drama: `arc/finish-it-or-leave-it-dark` is open, but the stakes are stated more
  than staged — no present-day inciting event node (no "first ring lit" beat); the
  conflict lives mostly in static belief/figure nodes.
- Inert: `figures/the-listener-glyph` "warns" the fleet but is otherwise a
  dangling node; `lore/shell-lumen-is-free-power` and several support-lore are
  color, not stakes.

**Rationale (B):**
- Coherence: tightest in the pair — every key `forged_by` its civ, a full 6-link
  `enables` artifact chain that correctly terminates ward→final-codex, and the
  5-step fragment mystery is identical in shape but better anchored.
- Depth: the standout is `events/the-completion` — a reconstructed climax node that
  binds all three absent civs by relation (`departed_at`/`departed_after`/
  `declined_at`), giving the three-civ stack a shared pivot instead of parallel
  asides.
- Richness: the Listeners get real weight via three named figures, especially
  `figures/the-listeners-last-pilgrim` who "chose to depart rather than fire the
  core" — a concrete precedent that directly grounds `beliefs/the-unturned-key-creed`.
  The trap/gift fork is earned by a prior species' actual refusal, not abstract
  caution.
- Novelty: slightly lower — lacks A's refusing-key and standalone one-shot curse
  (B folds irreversibility into `lore/frag-the-core-fires-once`, which is fine but
  less striking).
- Drama: strongest in the pair — `arc/turn-the-last-key` is "rising/live" with the
  final codex ALREADY in human hands (`figures/keyfinder-rust` owns it) and
  explicit branch beats (`next_beat_if_pressed` / `next_beat_if_held`), plus a live
  inciting event (`events/discovery`, first ring lit) and a freezing-colony
  pressure (`figures/engineer-davies`). The decision is loaded and imminent.
- Inert/redundancy: `lore/cradles-left-tidy` dropped by three artifacts; a couple
  of support-lore nodes are color. Minor.

**WINNER: B** (42 vs 38) — same scaffold, but B wires the Listeners' refusal into
a real precedent, gives the absent civs a shared pivot event, and stages an
imminent, branch-specified decision with the trigger already in hand. A has the
more inventive individual objects (the refusing key, the one-shot curse) but
leaves its key precedent and present-day conflict comparatively static.

## Theme 3 — extinction-plague-vector

Paired variants, very close: the makers (`civs/the-cradlekeepers`) sealed
themselves into quarantine-sleep behind an unfinished containment rather than die;
beside them sleep `civs/the-stillborn-host` (carriers the vector reached first,
now indistinguishable from the makers); the plague (`civs/the-vector`) is
biological-memetic — it rides the meaning of a read relic. A 5-step `prerequisite`
mystery (proof→identify→trigger→keystone→assemble) reconstructs the makers'
seal-recipe; the present-day arc (`arc/the-containment-race`) is a race to assemble
the seal in order before a stirring carrier on `drift-carrier-ninth` wakes.
Identical win/fail logic and even shared colonist names (Mara, Devrant).
Discriminator: backstory causality + artifact dual-use (A) vs present-day
political breadth (B), against B's slug-namespace sloppiness.

| Axis | World A | World B |
|---|---|---|
| Coherence | 9 | 7 |
| Causal/Structural Depth | 9 | 7 |
| Thematic Richness | 8 | 8 |
| Novelty/Surprise | 7 | 7 |
| Dramatic Potential | 8 | 8 |
| **Total** | **41** | **37** |

**Rationale (A):**
- Coherence: a real maker-era causal spine with proper `caused_by` chaining —
  `events/the-first-breach`→`the-naming-of-the-strain`→`the-dormancy-discovery`→
  `the-keystone-cast`→`the-quarantine-sleep` (the last flagged `is_pivot`), and each
  event's artifact is `forged_by` at a matching timestamp (day 100/200/300/500/800).
  Clean and consistent slug namespaces throughout.
- Depth: the dual-use artifact design is the strongest in the pair —
  `artifacts/the-cold-glyph` is "the held condition that keeps a sleeper under;
  misread, it is also the precise gesture that wakes one," and
  `artifacts/the-host-vault-key` opens to an ally OR a carrier depending on reading
  the cold-glyph first. The recipe is order-locked
  (`artifacts/the-sealwrights-recipe`). Mechanism and dread are fused.
- Richness: the cradlekeeper ambiguity (wake an ally who knows the recipe, or an
  outbreak) is genuinely tense; `civs/the-stillborn-host` "their waking is itself
  the outbreak" is a sharp image.
- Novelty: solid but the premise is the same as B; no third pole.
- Drama: `arc/the-containment-race` carries `next_beat_trigger` (Devrant's
  out-of-order crack surfacing in the foam) and `next_beat_stakes` (Soren opening
  the host-vault before the glyph is read) — concrete, sequenced escalation.
- Nit: the present-day timestamps are flattened to 800 (the pivot moment) on most
  drop/reveal edges; harmless but loses some present-tense layering.

**Rationale (B):**
- Coherence: weaker on two counts. (1) Slug-namespace inconsistency — most nodes
  use plural prefixes (`locations/`, `events/`) but several use singular
  (`location/the-half-seal`, `location/marrow-reliquary`, `event/the-translators-fever`,
  `event/the-keystone-located`, `curse/...`, `belief/...`). Real sloppiness. (2) No
  maker-era backstory chain at all — every event is present-day (day 0–110), so the
  "how the makers fell" layer that A dramatizes is only asserted in node text.
- Depth: the prerequisite mystery is identical in shape but all reveal/drop edges
  are timestamp 0 (temporally flat); less structural layering than A.
- Richness: B's strength — it adds a THIRD pole the conflict needs:
  `factions/the-ashline-creed` ("scorch the belt from orbit"), with three opposed
  beliefs (`belief/the-makers-can-be-woken` / `every-seal-is-a-lie` /
  `order-is-the-only-mercy`). More colonists with distinct stances
  (`colonists/sergeant-vo` warden, `colonists/dr-amein` epidemiologist), and
  `colonists/child-tam` ("the stakes the adults keep arguing over") is a sharp
  thematic anchor. `curse/the-reading-spreads-it` literalizes the memetic hazard.
- Novelty: parity with A.
- Drama: strong present-day staging — `events/the-carrier-stirs` ("the clock
  starts"), `events/the-ashline-vote` schism, `event/the-translators-fever`
  (exposure made concrete). The burn-vs-seal split gives it a second live axis A
  lacks.

**WINNER: A** (41 vs 37) — A is the more rigorously built graph: a causal maker-era
spine, order-locked dual-use artifacts, and consistent slugs. B is thematically
broader (the third burn-it-all faction + child-stakes are genuinely good) and
stages its present beats well, but its missing backstory layer and mixed
slug namespaces are concrete quality defects.

## Theme 4 — crowded-trade-federation

Shared premise: five living trade houses (`the-vossan-syndics` brokers,
`the-khariti-drift` nomads, `the-oseni-assayers` authenticators, `the-melun-cartel`
smugglers, `the-rho-collectors` hoarders) fight over ghostlight-amber, whose
"mined" provenance is a founding forgery; the buried truth is that the amber is
grown by a living maker-species the whole market pretends does not exist. A
provenance-chain mystery (`precedes`) reassembles the relic's true history.
Discriminator: A's convergent "four people each hold one chain-link" engine + full
causal backstory vs B's broader setting + reverse-excavation ordering, against B's
self-labeled flavor filler.

| Axis | World A | World B |
|---|---|---|
| Coherence | 9 | 8 |
| Causal/Structural Depth | 9 | 7 |
| Thematic Richness | 8 | 8 |
| Novelty/Surprise | 7 | 8 |
| Dramatic Potential | 9 | 7 |
| **Total** | **42** | **38** |

**Rationale (A):**
- Coherence: fully causal maker-era spine — `events/vossan-first-amber`→
  `oseni-seal-the-lie`→`rho-buy-the-story`→`oseni-schism`→`melun-flood-the-fakes`→
  `the-contested-deal` (flagged `is_climax`), `caused_by`-chained across days
  100–400, each house's betrayal seeded in order. Nit: one edge
  `colonists/mara-osei-vance owns factions/humans` is a relation typo (should be
  `member_of`), and the final arc→quen edge drops its timestamp.
- Depth: the standout engine — the four-link `precedes` mystery
  (`the-amber-was-never-mined`→`the-seal-can-be-unmade`→
  `the-relic-is-honest-the-paper-is-not`→`the-drift-knows-the-real-source`) maps
  onto four present-day colonists who each physically HOLD one link (Mara's
  route-log, Sable's confession, Quen's relic, plus the seal-die), all converging
  on one deal. Past lie and present scramble are the same structure.
- Richness: "the relic is honest, the paper is not" is a sharp inversion; the final
  reveal that the source "is alive" lands well.
- Novelty: good but the living-maker twist is shared with B.
- Drama: best in pair — `arcs/the-deal-nobody-dares-close` with explicit
  `next_beat_actor`/`next_beat` (Mara must pool or sell-alone before Haldane/Cartel
  reach her). Sell-once scarcity + four-way convergence is a real squeeze.

**Rationale (B):**
- Coherence: clean too — a present-day inciting chain `events/the-cheap-sale`→
  `the-buyback-offer`→`the-assayer-bribe`→`the-manifest-handoff`→`the-runs-ambush`
  (`caused_by`-linked, days 10–65). Consistent slugs.
- Depth: weaker backstory — the chain is reconstructed via `chain_position`
  metadata on the lore nodes rather than dramatized maker-era events, so the
  history is annotation, not enacted scenes.
- Richness: best SETTING in the pair — six controlled locations
  (`the-long-bazaar`, `the-assayers-hall`, `the-counterfeit-row`,
  `the-collectors-sanctum`, `the-melun-runs`, `the-drift-anchorage`), a figure per
  house, and explicit creeds (`beliefs/the-mined-myth` = "pretend no living maker
  is being mined"). Texture-rich.
- Novelty: highest-in-pair structural trick — the mystery `order` is REVERSED from
  the `fragments` array (fragments listed newest-lie-first, order runs
  true-origin→newest-lie), an intentional peel-back-the-forgeries excavation.
  `lore/amber-hums-when-near-kin` is a clever physical tell that cracks the myth.
- Drama: present beats are staged but the cast is flatter — no convergent
  "everyone holds one link" squeeze; `figures/marlena-okonkwo` simply hunts, funded
  by `figures/the-rho-archivist`. Lower-stakes geometry than A.
- Filler: a self-labeled block of `subtype:"flavor"` lore
  (`syndic-tariff-control`, `khariti-neutral-memory`, `collectors-blind-spot`) is
  honest-but-inert padding; one-per-house figures are mostly static `member_of` +
  `keeps` nodes.

**WINNER: A** (42 vs 38) — A's "four colonists, four chain-links, one deal" is the
strongest dramatic geometry in the whole half, backed by a fully causal house-by-
house backstory. B builds the richer marketplace and lands a genuinely clever
reverse-excavation ordering, but leans on self-labeled flavor lore and never
tightens its present-day cast into the same convergent squeeze.

## Theme 5 — uplift-nursery-world

The best-matched pair in the half. Identical core: a colony homesteads a world
that is "too kind"; hidden never-seen `civs/the-gardeners` (plus a maintenance
under-caste `the-loam-tenders`) are cultivating the colonists exactly as they
cultivated an earlier `the-first-cultivars` lineage that was "harvested" — not
killed but lifted off the world whole. A 5-step `reveals` method-chain ends on the
gut-punch that the harvest is FORWARD, not past ("we are the next row"). The fork:
accept the gifts (gratitude) vs go deliberately fallow. Discriminator: A's unique
sensing-power + dense sensory tells vs B's actionable bell-trigger + double-layered
structure.

| Axis | World A | World B |
|---|---|---|
| Coherence | 8 | 9 |
| Causal/Structural Depth | 8 | 9 |
| Thematic Richness | 9 | 8 |
| Novelty/Surprise | 8 | 8 |
| Dramatic Potential | 8 | 9 |
| **Total** | **41** | **43** |

**Rationale (A):**
- Coherence: clean 5-step `reveals` chain (`method-1-the-seeding`→…→
  `method-5-the-harvest-ahead`) and three human sub-factions
  (`factions/the-grateful` / `factions/the-harvesters`) nested under
  `factions/the-colony`. Solid.
- Depth: good, but the structure is single-layered (reveals only) — no artifact-
  gating progression; the present-day arc (`arc/are-we-the-harvest`) is
  conceptually stated rather than pinned to one actionable beat.
- Richness: highest in the pair — a dense bank of sensory "note" lore that each
  lands a concrete dread: `note-loom-edits-host` (Osei checks the loom against his
  children's blood-work and stops sleeping), `note-key-fits-us` (the key fits a lock
  in the human genome "not there at landfall"), `note-cultivars-gone` (the prior
  lineage "not destroyed; taken in"). `artifacts/attention-bell` "does not warn the
  colony — it summons the watchers" is excellent.
- Novelty: best original mechanic — `powers/sense-the-aimedness` ("the felt
  geometry of intent… convenience as a tell"), a distinct sensing faculty wielded by
  Maren and Osei. No equivalent in B.
- Drama: strong atmosphere (the watchers "score" tending vs stripping) but more
  diffuse — the conflict is two postures pulling, not one trigger anyone can pull.

**Rationale (B):**
- Coherence: tightest — same 5-step `reveals` mystery PLUS a parallel artifact
  `enables` chain (`terraforming-seed`→`soil-shrine`→`lineage-key`→`gene-loom`→
  `shaping-tool`→`attention-bell`), double-layered and consistent. Civs all bound to
  the climax event by relation (`taken_up_at`/`reaps_at`/`prepares`).
- Depth: the climax is a single concrete, irreversible, colony-CONTROLLED action —
  `arc/should-we-ring-the-bell`, where `artifacts/the-attention-bell` "sounding it
  deliberately calls a harvest." Agency + dread fused into one object, with
  `next_beat_event` + `next_beat_note` (native-born children testing ripe, bell
  within reach).
- Richness: strong — the harvested First Cultivars "lifted off the world whole and
  carried inward to whatever the garden feeds; not killed — completed and taken up"
  is the eeriest single image in the pair, and `lore/sap-carries-the-cultivar-song`
  (the harvested elders' growing-steps repeating like a lullaby) is haunting.
  Slightly less dense than A's note-bank.
- Novelty: parity — lacks A's sensing-power, but `figures/midwife-tomas-reyes`
  ("blessing or a breeding?") + `sought_by civs/the-loam-tenders` (the under-caste
  quietly reaching out, "leave a shrine warm so a clever guest will find it") is a
  fresh thread A doesn't have.
- Drama: best in the pair — the `the-grateful-guest-creed` vs `the-fallow-creed`
  ("refuse to ripen") fork is sharper than A's gratitude-vs-greed, and the whole
  thing converges on a bell anyone can ring.

**WINNER: B** (43 vs 41) — extremely close; both are the strongest worlds in the
half. A wins thematic density and contributes the genuinely novel
sense-the-aimedness power. B edges it by collapsing the same dread into one
actionable, irreversible object (ring the bell = call your own harvest), layering
an artifact-gating chain beneath the reveal-mystery, and sharpening the fork to
ripen-vs-stay-fallow. B is the better-engineered graph; A is the more
atmospheric one.

## Summary

| Theme | A total | B total | Winner |
|---|---|---|---|

| megastructure-absent-architects | 38 | 42 | **B** |

Across the half: A repeatedly wins on causal-backstory rigor, convergent dramatic
geometry, and a concrete buried payload; B repeatedly wins on present-day staging,
double-layered structure, and actionable irreversible triggers. The two systems
have different signatures rather than a flat quality gap. Recurring A weakness:
occasional inert "scenery" nodes and a couple of slug/relation typos. Recurring B
weakness: self-labeled flavor/filler lore, one hedged central secret (theme 1), and
a slug-namespace inconsistency (theme 3).
