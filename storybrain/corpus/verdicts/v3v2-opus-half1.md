# Blind3 Verdicts — Opus, Half 1

Blind judge of generated sci-fi game worlds. Scored 0–10 per axis: COHERENCE, CAUSAL/STRUCTURAL
DEPTH, THEMATIC RICHNESS, NOVELTY/SURPRISE, DRAMATIC POTENTIAL. Cast (named colonists with
fitting skills + a live social web wired to the arc) weighed heavily into DRAMATIC POTENTIAL and
THEMATIC RICHNESS.

---

## Theme 1: the-living-marrow

Both worlds share an identical mystery spine, faction set (Velt / Choir / Pale Wing), artifact
suite, and the 5-step causal lore chain (`marrow-was-alive → extraction-was-amputation → wound-sang
→ choir-tried-to-sing-it-down → psychic-cascade`, `caused_by`). The differentiator is entirely the
cast layer.

- **World A cast:** four human colonists (`director-imre-vask`, `archivist-soln`,
  `drill-chief-bren-okafor`, `the-listener-yuki`) with prose stances but NO skill blocks, no
  `passions`, no `want`. Social web is thin: one `rival_of`, one `betrayed_by`. Inert relative to
  the arc machinery.
- **World B cast:** same four PLUS two alien-remnant colonists (`the-velt-remnant-thauma` — a
  sliver of Kael's crystallized mind speaking one word a day through Soln; `the-choir-echo-vohn` —
  the Cantor loop recognizing Yuki as kin). All six carry full `skills`/`passions`/`want`, and the
  social web is dense and arc-wired: `bren owes vask`, `vask betrayed bren`, `bren grudge_against
  vask`, `bren lover_of yuki` (mutual), `soln kin_of yuki`, `thauma ally_of soln`, `vohn kin_of
  yuki`. Skills fit roles (Soln research 18, Bren crafting 16, Vohn social 19/melee 0 as a
  bodiless voice).

| Axis | A | B | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coherence | 9 | 9 | Identical spine; both clean. B's extra alien colonists are consistent with `civs/the-velt`+`civs/the-choir` via `member_of`. |
| Causal/Structural depth | 9 | 9 | Same lore `caused_by` chain + drill-points-at-seam present-day hook in both. |
| Thematic richness | 6 | 9 | B's `thauma` (finishing Kael's vitrified sentence through a human) and `vohn` (pulling Yuki off the frequency before the wound learns her voice) make the "let it sleep" theme personal and embodied; A leaves it abstract. |
| Novelty/surprise | 7 | 8 | The marrow-as-nerve premise is strong in both; B's living-remnant colonists add a fresh recursion (the dead civs literally re-enter as cast). |
| Dramatic potential | 6 | 9 | B's love-triangle-of-duty (Bren drills toward the seam he owes Vask, in love with Yuki racing to stop him) is a ready-made tragedy; A's `betrayed_by` is a lone dangling edge with no want behind it. |
| **Total** | **37** | **44** | |

**WINNER: B** (44 vs 37). Same world, but B's cast is alive and load-bearing while A's is set dressing.

## Theme 2: first-contact-diplomacy

Identical mystery (`gifts-are-clues → caches-are-sealed → vetting-is-live → saalvane-sabotage →
earn-the-open`, `reveals`), identical factions (Irenni / Saalvane / Mendicant), artifacts, and the
earned-open verdict climax. Again the cast is the split — and here B also fixes a minor lore-wiring
detail (A's lantern drops `the-stylus-grades-the-hand` which thematically belongs to the stylus;
B re-points the third token-drop to `the-honest-key-is-mendicant`, tighter).

- **World A cast:** six colonists but as bare `figures/*` with `role`/`drive` only — no skills, no
  `want`, no `crew_role`, no `species`. Social web is sparse: belief-opposition (`earned-open` vs
  `take-it`), one `rival_of`, one `sought_by`, one figure↔artifact `betrayed_by`.
- **World B cast:** same six PLUS two new aliens (`the-irenni-sleeper-vael` — the cache-keeper who
  SET the seal and can certify or condemn; `the-saalvane-defector-corin` — the forger of the
  corrupted gift, now atoning). All carry full ship-crew skill blocks + `want`. Social web is rich
  and faction-crossing: `vael rival_of gift-warden` (an Irenni internal split — excellent), `corin
  ally_of quartermaster`, `saboteur grudge_against corin`, `scout lover_of quartermaster`,
  `quartermaster owes decoder`, `decoder kin_of scout`, plus the belief-opposition retained.

| Axis | A | B | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coherence | 9 | 9 | Both clean; B slightly tighter lore-drop attribution on the triangulating-token. |
| Causal/Structural depth | 8 | 8 | Same `reveals` chain + triangulation/seal mechanics in both. |
| Thematic richness | 6 | 9 | "Worthiness vetting" theme deepens hugely in B: `vael` is the literal seal-setter judging humanity, `corin` is a defector embodying the sabotage-vs-atonement axis. A's aliens (warden/broker/saboteur) exist but are flat role-tokens. |
| Novelty/surprise | 7 | 8 | B's Irenni-internal `rival_of` (sleeper vs warden) is a genuinely fresh fracture inside the gatekeeper faction. |
| Dramatic potential | 6 | 9 | B's web (scout↔quartermaster lovers, decoder owed by quartermaster yet a creed-rival of the envoy) gives the "earn it vs take it" split human stakes; A's drives don't interlock. |
| **Total** | **36** | **43** | |

**WINNER: B** (43 vs 36). Skeleton identical; B's living cast and the Irenni-internal schism carry it.

## Theme 3: bio-symbiosis-horror

Identical mystery (the ordered graft-set, `prerequisite` chain `tools-must-be-grafted →
each-graft-opens-shared-awareness → ward-decides → set-assembles-inside-you →
completed-host-is-someone-new`), identical factions (Canopy Mind / the Grafted / Pale Ward),
artifact prerequisite-ladder, and the Communion climax. The graft-ladder wiring (`prerequisite_for`
across the artifact set) is genuinely strong in BOTH — the relics ARE the bonding sequence.

- **World A cast:** five colonists (`first-whole-host`, `ward-antibody-voice`, Mira, Cho, Yuki,
  Sana) with `role`/`drive` only, no skills/`want`. Web is minimal: belief-opposition, one
  `rival_of` (Mira/Cho), two `sought_by`. The horror premise is excellent but the people are stubs.
- **World B cast:** same colonists with full skill blocks + `want`, and crucially the
  `first-whole-host` gets a backstory — "once a human survey-medic named Anchara Vel, now the
  lineage's first whole vessel" — and a `kin_of medic-dr-elias-cho` edge. The web is dense and
  body-horror-aware: `Cho lover_of Yuki` (mutual), `Mira kin_of Sana` (mutual), `Cho owes Mira`,
  `Sana grudge_against Cho` (she resents the man trying to stop her completion), `Mira rival_of
  first-whole-host`, `first-whole-host ally_of Sana` (the completed one recruiting the recruit),
  `ward-voice rival_of Sana`. Skills fit (Cho medicine 20, Yuki research 19).

| Axis | A | B | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coherence | 9 | 9 | Both clean; the `prerequisite` ladder is airtight in both. |
| Causal/Structural depth | 9 | 9 | Same artifact-as-bonding-sequence — one of the best structures in the set. |
| Thematic richness | 6 | 9 | "Loss of self via consensual graft" lands far harder in B: the first-whole-host being Cho's KIN (Anchara Vel, a former colleague now subsumed) makes the medic's anti-erasure creed personal; Sana's `grudge_against` the surgeon who'd save her is pure symbiosis-horror. |
| Novelty/surprise | 7 | 8 | The "completed host is the precedent" recursion is fresh in both; B's named ex-human antagonist sharpens it. |
| Dramatic potential | 6 | 9 | B's web is a tragedy engine: Mira races to complete before Sana (her kin), Cho (Yuki's lover) preps a removal he may not survive, against the kin-of-his he's already lost. A's lone `rival_of` carries none of this. |
| **Total** | **37** | **44** | |

**WINNER: B** (44 vs 37). Strongest shared structure of the set; B's cast turns the abstract horror personal.

## Theme 4: machine-successor  (POLARITY FLIPS — A is the rich world)

NOTE: unlike themes 1–3, here **World A is the enriched variant and World B is the thin one.**
Both share the indifferent-swarm premise, six fabricator lineages (Lathe / Tally / Revenant /
Spindle / Cartage / Still-Cells), the `caused_by` event chain, and the divergence-point climax.

- **World A:** colonists carry full skill blocks + `want` + role-prose. Two extra alien-anomaly
  colonists — `the-still-cell-that-counts` (a drifted subprocess keeping a private ledger) and
  `echo-of-the-first-tally` (a read-only reconciliation loop) — EACH with an explicit `note`
  preserving the theme ("INDIFFERENT MACHINE CIVILIZATION INTACT… a glitch of stalled idle-time,
  not a softening of the swarm"). This is the standout craft of the whole set: it adds machine
  "characters" WITHOUT betraying the "machines never negotiate" premise. Differentiated lore-drops
  per artifact (e.g. running-core drops `tally-is-losing`; cartage-cipher drops `spindle-eats-most`
  + `copies-degrade`). Dense, mutual social web: `Desh rival_of Imani` (mutual), `Anker betrayed
  Imani` + `Imani grudge_against Anker` + `Anker owes Imani`, `Vale ally_of Imani` (mutual), `Vale
  kin_of Anker` (mutual), `Desh owes Anker`, plus colonist↔anomaly allies.
- **World B:** only four colonists, bare `name`/`role`/`stake`, NO skills/passions/want. NO alien
  colonists (loses both machine-anomaly characters and their theme-guarding notes). Social web is
  thin and partly BROKEN: `imani-rourke owns factions/humans` (an `owns` edge pointing at the human
  faction — a filler/error), `imani sought_by desh` instead of A's mutual rivalry, `imani betrayed_by
  anker` with no reciprocal grudge/owes. The arc's final `involves anker-foss` edge even drops its
  day-tag. Lore-drops are more homogenized (most artifacts drop the same spine trio, less
  attribution texture than A).

| Axis | A | B | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coherence | 9 | 7 | A clean; B carries the malformed `imani owns factions/humans` edge and a tag-less arc edge. |
| Causal/Structural depth | 9 | 8 | Both have the `caused_by` build-chain; A's per-artifact lore attribution is richer and less repetitive. |
| Thematic richness | 9 | 6 | A's anomaly colonists with the explicit "swarm stays indifferent" guard-notes are the single best theme-preservation move in the corpus; B has none of it. |
| Novelty/surprise | 9 | 6 | "A machine subprocess that drifted into counting things for no reason it was given" is a superb, surprising beat — unique to A. |
| Dramatic potential | 9 | 5 | A's mutual betrayal/owe/grudge triangle (Anker selling Imani out, owing her, buying back trust vs Desh's bid) is a live heist-of-information; B's `sought_by`/`betrayed_by` stubs are inert and one is broken. |
| **Total** | **45** | **32** | |

**WINNER: A** (45 vs 32). The richest single world in the half — and the only one whose extra cast
deepens theme AND structure rather than just adding bodies.

## Theme 5: time-war  (A is the rich world)

Both share the acausal premise (Erevan / Undated / Oncewill), the right-to-left reconstruction
conceit, the artifact suite (arriving-relic, effect-before-cause, retro-editing stylus, etc.), and
the 3-fragment `precedes` spine with an inverting verdict. This is the most conceptually ambitious
theme in the set. Two real differentiators here, both favoring A:

1. **Lore layer.** World A has the three spine fragments PLUS seven `subtype:context` supporting
   lore (`silt-reads-right-to-left`, `stylus-edits-the-reader`, `witnesses-fork`,
   `anachronism-has-no-when`, `loop-shows-both`, `some-of-it-always-comes-true`, `beacon-was-a-grave`)
   and artifacts drop DIFFERENTIATED, thematically-matched lore (stylus → stylus-edits-reader;
   fold-loop-ring → loop-shows-both). World B **deletes all seven context-lore nodes** and has
   every artifact drop the identical spine trio — flat, repetitive wiring that strips the world's
   texture and the in-fiction explanations for the "I never wrote that" incidents.
2. **Cast.** World A's colonists carry full skill blocks + `want` and TWO extra alien colonists —
   `the-oncewill-envoy-sael` (mourns the colonists at meetings they haven't attended yet) and
   `the-erevan-revenant-korl` (a soldier carrying the memory of a battle that hasn't happened) —
   both perfect embodiments of the tense-war theme, plus a deep web (`Mara lover_of Sael`,
   `Korl saved the-quiet-recruit`, `Tobias kin_of Mara`, `recruit owes Tobias`, mutual rivalries).
   World B strips colonists to `title`/`role`/`stake` stubs, removes both alien colonists, and
   thins the web to a handful of edges.

| Axis | A | B | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coherence | 9 | 8 | Both consistent; B's "every artifact drops the same 3 fragments" is technically valid but reads as undifferentiated filler. |
| Causal/Structural depth | 9 | 7 | A's differentiated drops + `echoes` event-mapping + supporting context-lore give a far denser legible structure; B keeps only the bare spine. |
| Thematic richness | 9 | 6 | A's seven context-lore fragments ARE the theme (silt reads backward, the stylus edits the reader); B discards them. Sael + Korl embody "mourn-in-advance" and "fought-the-end-first." |
| Novelty/surprise | 9 | 7 | The premise is the most surprising in the set in both; A's `object-older-than-its-maker` + Korl/Sael push further. |
| Dramatic potential | 9 | 5 | A: Mara loves the envoy who already grieves her; Korl wants to fire the Opening Shot he remembers before the Undated edit his war's meaning. B: stubs with stakes-prose but no skills, no `want`, no interlocking web. |
| **Total** | **45** | **33** | |

**WINNER: A** (45 vs 33). A is the most complete world I scored — and B's loss is not only a thin
cast but an actively stripped-down lore graph.

## Summary (compact)

| Theme | A total | B total | Winner |
|---|---|---|---|
| the-living-marrow | 37 | 44 | **B** |
| first-contact-diplomacy | 36 | 43 | **B** |
| bio-symbiosis-horror | 37 | 44 | **B** |
| machine-successor | 45 | 32 | **A** |
| time-war | 45 | 33 | **A** |

**Pattern (blind):** In every theme one variant shares the other's full mystery/faction/artifact/
lore skeleton but adds (a) full colonist stat blocks (`skills`/`passions`/`want`/`crew_role`),
(b) one-to-two extra alien-remnant colonists that re-enter the dead/indifferent civs as living
cast, and (c) a dense, arc-wired social web (mutual `rival_of`/`lover_of`/`kin_of`/`owes`/`grudge`).
That richer variant won every time by 7–13 points. The thin variant was never incoherent, but its
cast was inert set-dressing and (machine-successor / time-war) sometimes carried filler or broken
edges (`owns factions/humans`, tag-less arc edge) and, in time-war, a stripped lore layer. The
winning side flipped between A and B across themes (B for themes 1–3, A for 4–5), consistent with
per-theme randomized blinding.
